B. Accuracy against the synthetic reference process
====================================================================================================
'predicted range' is the min/max multiplier each method emitted over all test points and seeds
(a bounded range is a safety property: the flush can never be planned absurdly small or large).
Trainable methods fit on 200 noisy labelled examples (label noise sigma=10%),
mean over 10 training seeds. Test sets are noise-free. Errors are in multiplier units
(1.00 = the reference 10-day / 5 mm/hr flush). Shortfall = under-diverting, Excess = over-diverting.

At 5 minutes each observed tip equals 2.4 mm/hr, so all three controller bands are reachable.
